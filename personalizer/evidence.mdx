# Evidence

Figures and tables below come from two exploratory manuscripts:

1. **Resource ladder** — *When Should a Deployed BCI Decoder Spend Adaptation Resources?* (Paper 1)
2. **Universal Personalizer** — *One Personalizer Across Many Trunks* (Paper 1b)

All numbers are **exploratory**, protocol-local, and primarily locked to NimbusBench CSVs. Do not treat them as suite-wide constants.

**Headline (Paper 1b):** ship one Personalizer; treat fine-tune as a priced escape hatch. Use the head where capacity exists; stay cal-only when clean wins.

Labels **L0 / L1 / LP / L2 / L3** — see the [plain-language legend](/personalizer/overview#ladder-legend-plain-language). **Shipped path = L0/L1**; LP is a bench control; L2 is protocol/bench (affine); L3 is the FT hatch.

## Core: L0 / Bayesian head / linear probe / full FT

Main multi-trunk accuracy–cost block (Paper 1b): under the same ladder-transfer cells (severe feature shift, n=40, CUDA), cal-only Personalizer (L0), stream Personalizer (L1), a matched sklearn linear probe on the same frozen embeddings, and warm-start full FT (L3).

![Core: L0 / Bayesian head / linear probe / full FT (severe · n=40)](/images/personalizer-ft-trunks.png)

*Paper 1b — top: L0 / L1 / linear probe / L3 accuracy; blue `rec` labels = (acc_L1 − acc_L0) / (acc_L3 − acc_L0), or “≥FT” when L1 matches or beats L3. Bottom: adapt fit wall (L0 stream wall = 0) with L3/L1 ratios.*

| Dataset | Trunk | Acc L0 | Acc L1 | Acc LP | Acc L3 | Recovery | Wall L3/L1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNCI | EEGNet | 0.505 | 0.556 | 0.514 | 0.571 | 77% | ~33× |
| BNCI | Shallow | 0.495 | 0.572 | 0.580 | 0.553 | ≥FT | ~2× |
| BNCI | Deep | 0.497 | 0.572 | 0.536 | 0.596 | 76% | ~25× |
| BNCI | Conformer | 0.500 | 0.577 | 0.538 | 0.618 | 65% | ~33× |
| BNCI | ATCNet | 0.501 | 0.508 | 0.510 | 0.602 | 8% | ~41× |
| Zhou | EEGNet | 0.310 | 0.463 | 0.422 | 0.502 | 80% | ~21× |
| Zhou | Shallow | 0.390 | 0.425 | 0.445 | 0.543 | 23% | ~2× |
| Zhou | Deep | 0.338 | 0.467 | 0.462 | 0.482 | 90% | ~14× |
| Zhou | Conformer | 0.338 | 0.397 | 0.377 | 0.488 | 39% | ~31× |
| Zhou | ATCNet | 0.333 | 0.425 | 0.453 | 0.527 | 47% | ~39× |

Recovery = (acc_L1 − acc_L0) / (acc_L3 − acc_L0). On most trunks the Bayesian head recovers ~65–90% of the FT gain over cal-only and beats the matched probe (EEGNet / Deep / Conformer). Shallow and ATCNet are probe-competitive (LP can edge L1 by a few points) while both stay far below L3 wall — Personalizer remains the product default (same API + UQ / `BrainState`), not a claim that L1 always wins raw accuracy. BNCI Shallow is still the Pareto win vs FT (~2× wall); ATCNet on BNCI is the hatch case (8% recovery).

**Mid severity strengthens “head first”:** across BNCI+Zhou subject cells, stream L1 matches or beats warm-start L3 on accuracy in about half of cells at severity 0.25 / 0.5 (~49% / ~48%) versus about a third at 0.75 (~35%). Wall L3/L1 ratios stay expensive at every severity — severe is where FT most often still buys residual pp.

## Which head? (LDA vs QDA vs Softmax)

The multi-trunk map locks **LDA** for comparability. On the EEGNet path, Paper 1b also compares QDA and Softmax under the same bake-off geometry (severe and clean, n=40).

![EEGNet Personalizer head family: LDA vs QDA vs Softmax under severe shift](/images/personalizer-heads.png)

*Paper 1b — top: accuracy (Δacc vs LDA). Bottom: adapt fit wall with mean utility labels.*

| Dataset | Sev | Head | Acc | Wall (s) | ΔU vs LDA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNCI | 0.75 | LDA | 0.556 | 0.36 | — |
| BNCI | 0.75 | QDA | 0.644 | 0.36 | +0.085 |
| BNCI | 0.75 | Softmax | 0.589 | 0.59 | +0.031 |
| BNCI | 0.00 | LDA / QDA / Softmax | ≈0.71 | 0.35–0.71 | ≈0 |
| Zhou | 0.75 | LDA | 0.462 | 0.43 | — |
| Zhou | 0.75 | QDA | 0.452 | 0.43 | −0.010 |
| Zhou | 0.75 | Softmax | 0.457 | 0.71 | −0.005 |

- **Product default:** LDA (`head="lda"`) — no optional dependency; locked ladder / FT narrative.
- **BNCI severe:** QDA lifts utility (~+0.085–0.088) at essentially the same wall; Softmax gains accuracy but pays ~1.7× LDA wall.
- **Zhou severe (n=4):** inconclusive — QDA / Softmax at or below LDA. Clean BNCI/Zhou: heads nearly tied on U.
- Softmax stays optional (`nimbus-bci[softmax]`) for UQ-oriented deployments. The 18-cell transfer map is not re-run for every head.

API knobs: [Encoder contract](/personalizer/encoder-contract).

## Product claim (honest boundaries)

| Regime | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factory works | Clean / weak natural transfer | Stay cal-only (L0) — 13/18 cells |
| Factory breaks | Mid / severe controlled stress | Stream head (L1) often lifts utility; usually beats matched linear probe |
| Full retrain unnecessary | Same budgets as stress recovery | Classical: L1 recovers most of L3 at ~14–40× wall; EEGNet BNCI head can beat FT (~5× bake-off wall); REVE ~20–40× vs short FT |
| Head choice | Same Personalizer API | Default LDA; QDA optional under severe shift (BNCI); Softmax optional for UQ |

Not claimed: L1 always beats L0 or LP; QDA always beats LDA; universal ordinal severity→level law; coverage of session / electrode / SNR shift (evidence is synthetic feature shift); foundation SOTA; suite-wide 30× ratios from other protocols; shipped “Active Inference for EEG”; shipped affine-Z (L2) as a Personalizer API.
