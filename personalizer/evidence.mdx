# Evidence

Figures and tables below come from two exploratory manuscripts:

1. **Resource ladder** — *When Should a Deployed BCI Decoder Spend Adaptation Resources?* (Paper 1)
2. **Universal Personalizer** — *One Personalizer Across Many Trunks* (Paper 1b)

All numbers are **exploratory**, protocol-local, and primarily locked to NimbusBench CSVs. Do not treat them as suite-wide constants.

**Headline (Paper 1b):** ship one Personalizer; treat fine-tune as a priced escape hatch. Use the head where capacity exists; stay cal-only when clean wins.

## Where the head helps (severity sweep)

Stream Personalizer (L1) vs cal-only (L0) across synthetic feature-shift severities on an 18-cell trunk×dataset map:

| Severity | Cells with ΔU(L1−L0) > 0 |
| --- | --- |
| 0 (clean) | **4 / 18** |
| 0.25 | **13 / 18** |
| 0.50 | **16 / 18** |
| 0.75 (severe) | **12 / 18** |

*Paper 1b — mid shift already recovers; severe is a detailed slice, not the only supporting point. Kumar stays near zero (boundary).*

### Severe winners (mean utility)

At severity 0.75, which ladder level wins by mean utility (L0 cal-only / L1 stream head / L2 affine+head):

| Dataset | n | EEGNet | Shallow | Deep | Conformer | ATCNet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNCI | 9 | L2 | L2 | L1 | L2 | L0 |
| Zhou | 4 | L2 | L1 | L1 | L2 | L2 |
| Kumar | 18 | L0 | L2 | — | L0 | — |
| Shin | 9 | L1 | L0 | L1 | L2 | L0 |

Zhou: head helps on every trunk. BNCI: often useful, not automatic (ATCNet stays L0). Kumar/Shin: boundaries — Personalizer is the default *integration* path, not a guarantee that L1 beats L0 everywhere.

## Cost bake-off (EEGNet, BNCI primary)

Under severe feature shift with n=40 labeled stream trials, a Bayesian head recovers most of the full fine-tune accuracy gain at a fraction of the adaptation fit wall.

![BNCI2014-004 cost bake-off: Bayesian head accuracy vs adaptation fit time compared to frozen, linear probe, LoRA, last-layer FT, and full FT](/images/personalizer-cost.png)

*Paper 1 — BNCI2014-004, severity 0.75, n=40 (mean over 9 subjects).*

| Arm | Acc | ECE | Fit time (s) | Recovery vs full FT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen EEGNet | 0.476 | 0.333 | 0 | — |
| **Bayesian head** | **0.553** | **0.136** | **0.35** | **0.73** |
| Linear probe | 0.517 | 0.142 | 0.35 | 0.40 |
| LoRA | 0.502 | 0.200 | 1.08 | 0.26 |
| Last-layer FT | 0.472 | 0.323 | 1.31 | — |
| Full FT | 0.582 | 0.205 | 1.78 | 1.00 |

Recovery = Δacc / Δacc_fullFT. On Zhou2016 (supporting, 4 subjects) the same cell is softer (~46% recovery at ~5.5× lower fit time).

![EEGNet Personalizer versus fine-tune arms: accuracy and adaptation fit time on BNCI and Zhou](/images/personalizer-ft.png)

*Paper 1b — EEGNet severe n=40; Bayesian head vs full FT.*

| Dataset | Acc frozen | Acc head | Acc FT | Recovery | Bake-off wall FT/head |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNCI | 0.476 | 0.553 | 0.582 | 73% | **5.0×** |
| Zhou | 0.352 | 0.430 | 0.523 | 46% | **5.5×** |

Bake-off walls (~5×) and ladder-transfer CUDA L3/L1 ratios (~20–30×) differ because L1 walls are millisecond-scale in the transfer protocol — same qualitative story: FT is the expensive hatch.

## Foundation REVE vs short fine-tune

The same Personalizer contract runs on Hub REVE. Under severe shift, adapt wall stays sub-second while last-layer / full FT cost about **20×–40×** more on Zhou/Kumar.

![REVE Personalizer versus short fine-tune: Δacc and adapt fit time on Zhou and Kumar](/images/personalizer-reve-ft.png)

*Paper 1b — REVE severe n=40; accuracy × price.*

| Dataset | Acc frozen | Acc head | Acc last | Acc full | Wall last/head | Wall full/head |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zhou | 0.365 | 0.412 | 0.498 | 0.393 | **21×** | 47× |
| Kumar | 0.490 | 0.548 | 0.541 | 0.512 | **24×** | 42× |

- **Kumar:** head Pareto-dominates short FT (+5.8 pp vs last +5.1 pp at far lower wall).
- **Zhou:** last-layer can buy extra pp (+13 vs head +4.7) at ~21× wall — priced purchase, not the default.
- **Not claimed:** frozen REVE + Personalizer matches cal-trained EEGNet absolute accuracy. BNCI REVE cost is a 3-subject smoke — not a headline.

## Transfer across trunks

Soft operational default vs strict ordinal schedule (18 cells; exploratory):

| Slice | Clean → L0 | Severe → L2 | Strict ordinal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zhou | 5/5 | 3/5 | 3/5 |
| BNCI | 4/5 | 3/5 | 2/5 |
| Kumar | 3/3 | 1/3 | 1/3 |
| Shin | 1/5 | 1/5 | 0/5 |
| **Pooled** | **13/18** | **8/18** | **6/18** |

![Transfer map for the Personalizer adaptation ladder across 18 trunk×dataset cells](/images/personalizer-transfer-map.png)

*Paper 1b — cal-only-when-clean transfers often (13/18); strict ordinal (clean→L0 and severe→L2) is conditional (6/18) — boundary, not the product headline.*

## Product claim (honest boundaries)

| Regime | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factory works | Clean / weak natural transfer | Stay cal-only (L0) — 13/18 cells |
| Factory breaks | Mid / severe controlled stress | Stream head (L1) often lifts utility |
| Full retrain unnecessary | Same budgets as stress recovery | EEGNet head ≈ most of FT Δacc at ~5× bake-off wall; REVE ~20–40× cheaper than short FT |

Not claimed: L1 always beats L0; universal ordinal severity→level law; coverage of session / electrode / SNR shift (evidence is synthetic feature shift); foundation SOTA; suite-wide 30× ratios from other protocols; shipped “Active Inference for EEG.”
